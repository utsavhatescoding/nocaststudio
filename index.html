Before you order

Clear answers for small businesses.

What photo should I send?+

Send a clear front-facing photo with the complete garment visible. Good light and a plain background improve the result, but a professional camera is not required.

Will logos and garment details stay accurate?+

We review colour, logo, pattern, neckline, sleeves and garment length. AI can still change very small details, so you approve the result before receiving the final file.

Do I need an account or subscription?+

No. For the pilot service, order directly through WhatsApp. We confirm the garment, model, price and delivery time before beginning paid work.

Can you handle kurta, suits and local tailoring?+

Yes, when the complete garment is clearly visible. Complex embroidery, drapes and unusual cuts may require extra review and are quoted after seeing the input photo.
